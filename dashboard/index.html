Does history help—and does a neural network add value?

01

History helps

Engineered rolling statistics and slopes outperform snapshot-only models on FD002 and the frozen FD004 replication.

02

Conditions help modestly

Explicit operating regimes improve aggregate snapshot MAE, but the FD002 confidence interval includes zero.

03

Complexity is not automatically value

The Temporal CNN is competitive on FD002 but falls behind engineered history on the held-back FD004 evaluation.

Lower MAE is better. Phase 2 and FD004 give every training engine equal total weight. FD002 Phase 2 was designed after earlier test results were reviewed; FD004 methods were fixed before its test labels were opened.
